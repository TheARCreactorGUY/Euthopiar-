Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about privacy, integrations, and plugin architecture before installing.

Read Privacy Policy

Is Euthopiar free and open source? Yes. Euthopiar is completely free, contains zero advertisements, and its source code is publicly auditable on GitHub.

How does Trakt and Simkl synchronization work? Connections use official OAuth 2.0 PKCE flows directly between your phone and Trakt or Simkl servers. OAuth tokens remain in local private app storage and never pass through third-party proxies.

Does Euthopiar host or distribute video streams? No. Euthopiar is strictly a media manager and player interface. Users configure their own custom or community provider manifests in Settings under Plugins.

Does Euthopiar track what I watch? No. Playback progress, watchlist caches, and app preferences stay locally on your device. Read our dedicated Privacy Policy for complete details.

Can I save movies and episodes for offline travel? Yes. The built-in download manager supports background downloads, pause and resume queue recovery, and an Offline-Only toggle in the Library tab.

What Android version is required? Euthopiar runs on Android 8.0 (Oreo) and higher across modern phones and tablets, with Media3 ExoPlayer hardware decoding and Picture-in-Picture support.
