EUTHOPIAR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Euthopiar is an open-source, client-side Android application designed for media discovery, personal watchlist management, and viewing history tracking. We respect your privacy and operate with strictly on-device storage and direct service connections.

1. Information Collection & Storage

  • Zero Personal Data Collection: Euthopiar does not collect, log, sell, or transmit your personal information, IP addresses, or viewing history to any developer-operated analytics or tracking servers.
  • On-Device Storage: All app preferences, cached artwork metadata, and local playback progress remain strictly on your device's local storage.
  • Open-Source Transparency: The application source code is publicly auditable on GitHub.

2. Third-Party Integrations & Authentication

You may optionally connect third-party tracking and metadata services directly from your device:

A. Trakt.tv Integration

  • Authentication (OAuth 2.0 PKCE): Connecting your Trakt.tv account uses Trakt's official OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow. Euthopiar never collects or stores your Trakt password.
  • Token Storage: Your Trakt OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your device in private application storage solely to authorize requests you initiate.
  • Direct API Requests: Watchlist synchronization, history updates, and playback scrobbles are transmitted directly from your device to Trakt's official API (https://api.trakt.tv).

Euthopiar uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

B. Simkl Integration

  • Authentication: Uses Simkl's official OAuth 2.0 PKCE authorization flow.
  • Data Transmission: Watch progress and lists sync directly between your device and Simkl's official API.

C. Metadata & Optional Cloud Backup

  • TMDB & Subtitles: Media posters, descriptions, and optional subtitles are fetched directly from third-party APIs (such as The Movie Database) based on titles you browse or watch.
  • Optional User-Initiated Backup: If you choose to sign in for personal cloud library sync, synchronization runs only for your personal account data.

3. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You stay in full control of your data and can disconnect at any time:

  1. Inside Euthopiar: Go to Settings → Integrations and tap Disconnect to immediately remove stored OAuth tokens from your device.
  2. From Trakt.tv: Revoke access at any time from your Trakt Connected Apps settings page.
  3. Local Data: Clearing app data or uninstalling Euthopiar removes all locally stored preferences, cache, and tokens.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions or requests regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub Issues page.
